# Fluxvell Contact

**URL:** https://fluxvell.com/contact

> Let's Secure Your Vendor Ecosystem

The page invites organizations to use intelligent third-party risk management to evaluate vendors, strengthen compliance, or explore enterprise partnerships. It says the Fluxvell team is available to help.

## Contact details and form

The page provides the email link **sales@fluxvell.com** under the text **Email us anytime**.

| Field | Requirement or instruction shown on the page |
|---|---|
| Full Name | Required |
| Email Address | Required |
| Phone Number | Minimum 10 digits |
| Company Name | No additional requirement shown |
| Interested In | Required; options are External Attack Surface Management, Cyber Threat Intelligence, Third-Party Risk Management, Digital Risk Protection, and Other |
| Message | Required; minimum 10 characters |

The form action is **Send Message**. The page states that submission constitutes agreement to the Privacy Policy.

## Closing CTA

The page asks, **Ready to See Fluxvell in Action?** It invites visitors to **Book a personalized architecture walk-through and discover how our platform scales security operations.** The action is **Book a Demo**.

## Site navigation

The page header presents **Home**, **Solutions**, **Products**, **Industries**, **Partners**, **Contact Us**, and **Request Demo**. The Solutions, Products, and Industries labels operate as navigation menus on the live site.

## Footer content

The inner-page footer groups links under **Products**, **Solutions**, and **Company**, and displays certification labels. The Products links are Third-Party Risk Management, Brand Reputation Management, Operational Risk Management, Dark Web Surveillance, and Cyber Insurance. The Solutions links are Vendor Risk Management, Attack Surface Management, Threat Intelligence, Vulnerability Management, and Executive Monitoring. The Company links shown on inner pages are About Us, Careers, Contact, and Blog, although some pages display only Contact and Blog. The certification labels shown are SOC 2 Certified, ISO 27001 Certified, and GDPR Compliant unless the page-specific extraction shows a sector-specific certification.
