> Monitor and manage operational risks with comprehensive insights into financial data, legal history, litigation exposure, and M&A activity across your vendor ecosystem.

## Live risk matrix display

The page presents **System: Live Risk Matrix**. Displayed examples include **High Risk / Review**, **FIN-04 / Credit Runoff / 88.4%**, **Alert / Litigation Risk / Acme Class-Action / Docket Entry #412**, and **MA-90 / Structural M&A / Pending / Ownership Shift**. ## Overview

The overview heading is **Comprehensive Operational Risk Intelligence**. Axentro Operational Risk Management is described as providing deep insights into the financial health, legal exposure, litigation history, and M&A activity of vendors and partners to enable proactive risk mitigation. The labels **Financial Monitoring** and **Legal Risk Tracking** are shown.

The page lists **Identify Financial Risks Early**, **Monitor Legal Exposure**, **Track M&A Activity**, and **Protect Business Continuity**. The descriptions cover detecting financial instability before impact, staying ahead of litigation and regulatory risks, understanding ownership changes, and safeguarding operations through informed decisions.
