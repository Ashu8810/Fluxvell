## Key security challenges

**OT/ICS Vulnerabilities:** Aging industrial control systems are increasingly targeted. **Supply Chain Risks:** Equipment vendors and service providers introduce vulnerabilities. **Regulatory Compliance:** Organizations must meet NERC CIP, ISO 27001, and other requirements.

## How Fluxvell secures energy companies

**OT Asset Monitoring** continuously monitors OT assets, ICS, and critical infrastructure for threats. **Vendor Risk Management** assesses and monitors equipment vendors, service providers, and contractors. **Threat Intelligence** provides real-time energy-sector intelligence, including OT/ICS-specific attacks and nation-state threats. **Executive Reporting** generates executive-ready reports and dashboards for regulatory compliance and stakeholder communication.
