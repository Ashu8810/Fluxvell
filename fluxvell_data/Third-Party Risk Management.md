## How it works

The page describes a four-stage workflow from onboarding to ongoing oversight. **Step 01 — Onboard vendors:** import a vendor list via CSV, API, or single sign-on in minutes. **Step 02 — Automated assessment:** send CAIQ/SIG questionnaires and auto-score submitted evidence with AI. **Step 03 — Continuous monitoring:** track attack surface, dark web exposure, and compliance drift in real time. **Step 04 — Remediate & report:** assign remediation tasks to vendors and export audit-ready reports on demand.

## FAQ

The page defines third-party risk management as identifying, assessing, and monitoring risks introduced by vendors, suppliers, and other external parties with access to data, systems, or operations. It says automated scoring uses standardized CAIQ and SIG questionnaires, cross-references evidence with external threat intelligence and public data sources, and produces weighted scores across cybersecurity, financial, compliance, and operational categories.

The listed supported frameworks are SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST CSF, GDPR, and HIPAA, with custom questionnaires available. The page says most teams can import an existing vendor list and send a first assessment within a day, while vendors typically return questionnaires and evidence within three to five business days. It says Axentro connects with tools such as Jira, ServiceNow, Okta, and Slack so findings flow into existing workflows.
