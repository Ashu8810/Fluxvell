## Hero

The homepage is headed **External Attack Surface Monitoring**. It invites visitors to discover and monitor their external exposure, receive real-time threat intelligence, and mitigate cyber risks with an AI-powered platform. The hero includes a **Request Demo** call to action and the trust badges **Enterprise Grade Security**, **Scalable**, and **AI-Powered**.

The homepage describes its product area as **Complete risk visibility across your entire ecosystem**.

| Product | Description shown on the homepage |

| Brand Risk Protection | Monitor threats to your brand across the web, social, and dark web. |
| Third Party Risk Management | Assess, monitor, and reduce risks in your vendor and partner ecosystem. |

A **View All Products** action is shown.

## One Platform. Complete Risk Visibility.

The homepage says, **See more, understand faster, and act with confidence.** The Complete Risk Visibility section says the platform consolidates security data into a single pane of glass, providing visibility across the entire ecosystem. The listed capabilities are to monitor third-party vendors and their risk scores, track high-, medium-, and low-risk vendors, identify top risk drivers across the organization, and view risk trends to prioritize actions. A **See Product in Action** action accompanies a Fluxvell dashboard visual.
