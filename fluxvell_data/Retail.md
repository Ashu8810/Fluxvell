## Overview

The overview heading is **Securing Retail Operations**. Retailers are described as facing challenges from high-volume transactions, customer data, and complex supply chains. **Payment Security** protects payment gateways and detects checkout anomalies. **Omnichannel Protection** provides unified security for brick-and-mortar and e-commerce. The section headline is **Customer Trust**, described as protecting sensitive customer data, preventing breaches, and building lasting trust.

## Key security challenges

**Payment Fraud** includes card-not-present fraud, automated bot checkouts, and chargebacks. **Customer Data Breaches** threaten loyalty programs and checkout databases containing PII and payment data. **Supply Chain Risks** arise from logistics vendors, suppliers, and integrated POS software providers.
