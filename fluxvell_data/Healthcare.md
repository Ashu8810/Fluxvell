## Key security challenges

**Patient Data Breaches:** Sensitive patient information is a prime target. **Medical Device Security:** Connected medical devices introduce vulnerabilities and attack vectors. **Regulatory Compliance:** Organizations must meet HIPAA, GDPR, and other healthcare requirements.

## How Fluxvell secures healthcare providers

**Patient Data Protection** monitors and protects patient records, EHRs, and sensitive healthcare data. **Medical Device Security** monitors and secures connected medical devices, IoT devices, and healthcare infrastructure. **Threat Intelligence** provides real-time healthcare-specific intelligence, including ransomware and medical-device attacks. **Executive Reporting** generates executive-ready reports and dashboards for compliance and stakeholder communication.
