## Key security challenges

**Student Data Breaches:** Sensitive student information and records are prime targets. **Phishing Attacks:** Students and faculty are frequent targets of phishing campaigns. **Research Security:** Valuable research data and intellectual property need protection.

## How Fluxvell secures education institutions

**Student Data Protection** monitors and protects student PII and academic records. **Phishing Protection** detects and prevents phishing attacks targeting students and faculty. **Research Security** protects research data and intellectual property. **Executive Reporting** generates reports for boards, administrators, and stakeholders.
