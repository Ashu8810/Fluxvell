## Continuous compliance and risk visibility

The page describes Axentro Vendor AI as supporting automated assessments and tailored evaluation templates for a comprehensive, proactive approach to vendor risk management. Its capability cards are **AI-powered Assessments**, **Adaptive Questionnaires**, **Seamless Collaboration**, and **Customizable Templates**.

## Transform vendor risk assessments into actionable intelligence

The page presents three steps: **Industry-Specific Risk Assessments**, in which CAIQ/SIG templates are used to customize questionnaires; **Adaptive Vendor Risk Visibility**, in which questionnaires are automatically distributed when inconsistencies are flagged in real time; and **Seamless Vendor Collaboration**, in which everything is consolidated in one platform for tracking and management.
