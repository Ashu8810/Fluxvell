## Key security challenges

**Guest Data Breaches:** Sensitive guest information and payment data are prime targets. **Guest Wi-Fi Security:** Unsecured guest networks and connected devices create vulnerabilities. **Property Management:** Property-management systems and IoT devices introduce attack vectors.

## How Fluxvell secures hospitality organizations

**Guest Data Protection** protects guest PII, payment information, and reservations across properties and systems. **Wi-Fi Security** monitors and secures guest Wi-Fi networks, connected devices, and IoT systems. **Threat Intelligence** provides hospitality-specific intelligence, including fraud attacks and data breaches. **Executive Reporting** generates reports and dashboards for property management and stakeholders.
