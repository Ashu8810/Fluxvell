## Key security challenges

**Content Piracy:** Digital piracy and unauthorized distribution of premium content. **IP Theft:** Intellectual-property theft and unauthorized content access. **Distribution Security:** Securing content-distribution channels and streaming platforms.

## How Fluxvell secures media organizations

**Content Protection** monitors and protects premium content from piracy, unauthorized distribution, and digital theft. **IP Protection** protects intellectual property, scripts, music, and creative assets from cyber threats and unauthorized access. **Threat Intelligence** provides media-and-entertainment-specific intelligence, including piracy and IP-theft patterns. **Executive Reporting** generates reports and dashboards for content executives and stakeholders.
